1. Overview of Visa VAMP 2026 Updates

Visa System Integrity Monitoring Program (VAMP) imposes strict compliance thresholds on merchant chargebacks and fraud ratios. Failing to meet these metrics exposes high-risk merchants to immediate acquirer fines and processor termination.

Critical Metric: Exceeding a 0.9% chargeback-to-transaction ratio for two consecutive cycles triggers automatic placement in the Visa Acquirer Monitoring Program.
