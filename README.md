## ⚖️ Legal & Regulatory Foundations

### 1. UCC § 4A-502 & Bank Setoff Limitations
Under **Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) § 4A-502**, a payment processor or acquiring bank may only hold merchant funds strictly necessary to cover quantifiable liabilities. Indefinite retention of 100% of receipts when chargeback ratios are below card scheme monitoring thresholds (1.0%) constitutes bad-faith conversion and breach of contract.

### 2. Visa VAMP (2026) & Mastercard ANCRM Thresholds
- **Visa VAMP (2026 Rules):** Unified monitoring triggers at **0.9% dispute ratio** and **$75,000 monthly fraud volume**.
- **Mastercard ANCRM Framework:** Tier 1 monitoring at **1.0% / 100 chargebacks**; Tier 2 at **1.5% / 300 chargebacks** with escalating monthly risk assessments.

### 3. FTC Strict Liability & Merchant Compliance
Under recent FTC enforcement standards, processors are legally pressured to apply zero-tolerance automated holds. Merchants must maintain formal KYC/AUP compliance dossiers and multi-entity risk isolation.
